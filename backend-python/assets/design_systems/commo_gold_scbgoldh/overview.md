A royal-purple-and-gold wealth aesthetic built on deep violet backgrounds and dramatically lit gold product photography. The composition is diagonal: a polished gold bar rises from the right third on a pile of gold coins while a frosted-translucent info card anchors the left with product details, and a Thai sans headline sits directly on the flat purple above. Typography is bold Thai sans for body in pure white, with the fund name treated as a gold condensed display wordmark set in all caps with letterpress weight. Studio lighting is dramatic — a single hot key on the gold creates rich specular highlights, and a radial purple gradient deepens toward the corners to give institutional gravitas. Text-image relationship uses glassmorphic frosted cards over the busy hero for product-detail readability, and direct overlay for the headline where no card is used. Negative space is generous at around thirty percent, giving the product room to breathe. A single purple pill CTA sits centered near the bottom with fine disclaimer print below it. The emotional register is institutional, prestigious, solid, and wealth-aspirational — not playful, not consumer-casual, not lifestyle-warm. No pastel palette, no flat lighting, no secondary chromatic accents besides gold, no crowded composition.
