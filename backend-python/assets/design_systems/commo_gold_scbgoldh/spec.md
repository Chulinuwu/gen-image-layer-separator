# Royal Purple Wealth

## A. Visual Mood
Luxurious, institutional, prestigious, serious-confident. The visual language of wealth accumulation — deep royal purple for stability and value, rich gold for tangible reward. Energy is not playful or aspirational in the consumer-lifestyle sense; it is institutional, solid, arriving. Mood keywords: luxurious, institutional, prestigious, stable, wealth.

## B. Color Story
- dominant: #3F1F6C (deep royal purple) — gradient background primary
- accent: #D4AF37 (rich gold) — product hero color, numerical emphasis
- text_primary: #FFFFFF (pure white) — headline and body set on purple
- text_secondary: #E9DAF8 (light lilac) — fine print and disclaimer over purple
- shadow: #1E0B3C (near-black purple) — darkest gradient corner and vignette
- highlight: #F5D870 (pale warm gold) — fund wordmark letter highlights, price accents
- palette_relationship: complementary pairing (deep purple with warm gold) — the classic institutional wealth pairing; no tertiary chromatic accents

## C. Typography Personality
- font_character: bold Thai sans display for the headline; fund name set as all-caps condensed gold wordmark with letterpress weight
- weight_hierarchy: headline 700, fund name 800 condensed, body 500, disclaimer 400
- tracking_behavior: tight at display, wider at fund name to carry weight, normal at body
- text_treatments: gold fill with subtle inner shadow on the fund name; clean white on headlines; no strokes; a frosted translucent rounded card serves as container for product detail text

## D. Composition Archetype
- focal_pattern: diagonal — gold bar hero anchors the right third rising up, info card balances the left, coins occupy bottom edge
- text_zones: headline top-left, product info card left-center, CTA pill bottom-center, fine-print strip at the very bottom
- negative_space_ratio: ~30% — generous breathing room, product given space
- subject_position: right third, tilted three-quarter diagonal

## E. Scene / Photo Style
- lighting_direction + quality: dramatic studio — single bright key on gold bar creating hot specular highlights; soft rim from below on coin pile
- depth: shallow DoF — coins slightly soft, purple field is flat gradient
- environment: studio product shot on colored seamless; no real-world context
- texture: hyper-polished gold metal, matte purple field, stacked coin clutter at base

## F. Subject Treatment
- posing: gold bar tilted three-quarter view, coins piled as supporting texture
- scale: hero-dominant — bar occupies about 40% of canvas
- cropping: full bar in frame; bottom coins intentionally bleed off the edge

## G. Text-Image Relationship
- pattern: overlay with a frosted translucent card — card provides a soft glassmorphic surface for product details over the busy purple-and-gold scene
- contrast_strategy: frosted card (white rgba at ~8%, blurred backdrop) plus solid white body text for product details; headline floats directly on flat purple with no card

## H. Effects Library
- gradients: radial purple gradient on background (lighter mid-plane, darker corners); vertical gold gradient on bar faces; subtle gold glow halo around the bar silhouette
- glows: soft golden ambient behind the bar; small specular sparkles on individual coin edges
- strokes: none visible
- drop_shadows: deep purple shadow under bar and coin pile to ground them on the gradient field

## I. Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Anchor every layout on deep royal purple and rich gold exclusively
- Use dramatic directional studio lighting on the hero product
- Keep text containers as frosted translucent cards over the busy scene
- Reserve gold strictly for brand or product names and emphasis numerals

Don't:
- Don't introduce pastels, teals, or low-saturation neutrals
- Don't use flat front lighting — the gold needs specular drama
- Don't put the headline inside the info card — card is for product detail only
- Don't combine gold with any other chromatic accent (no red, no blue, no green, no orange)
- Don't use lifestyle photography — this is a studio product aesthetic
