# Cheerful Beach Family

## A. Visual Mood
Cheerful, outdoor-carefree, protective-warm. The emotional register is optimistic family-vacation — children play safely because parents chose protection. Energy is bright, daytime, buoyant. Mood keywords: cheerful, outdoor, family-safe, sunny, optimistic.

## B. Color Story
- dominant: #8AD1EA (sky aqua) and #C8E8F2 (pale cloud blue) — environmental palette split across sky
- accent: #FFC93C (sunny yellow) — highlights, CTA button fill, promo numerals
- text_primary: #1C2858 (deep navy) — all body text and titles
- text_secondary: #2E4E7E (mid navy blue) — product description subheads
- shadow: none used as a color role — environment is inherently bright
- highlight: #47B680 (check-mark green) — used only on feature-bullet check circles
- palette_relationship: analogous cool sky blues anchored by a single warm yellow accent punch, with teal-green reserved for iconography

## C. Typography Personality
- font_character: rounded friendly Thai sans with open counters and soft corners
- weight_hierarchy: headline 800, card headline 700, body 500, price numerals 800 display
- tracking_behavior: normal throughout; no aggressive compression at display sizes
- text_treatments: solid yellow fill on a handful of emotional emphasis phrases within the headline; no stroke; no shadow on card text; faint soft drop shadow behind the floating headline so it lifts off the sky

## D. Composition Archetype
- focal_pattern: right-weighted two-column — lifestyle photo occupies the right 50%, a white product card sits on the left 45%, and the headline bridges across the top
- text_zones: title band top, product card left-center, bottom strip for disclaimer and CTA
- negative_space_ratio: ~20% — dense but organized
- subject_position: right-weighted, family occupies the right 45%, standing vertical on sand

## E. Scene / Photo Style
- lighting_direction + quality: bright natural daytime sun from upper-left, soft warm cast on subjects, open shade balance
- depth: deep focus — everything sharp across frame, no shallow DoF
- environment: outdoor beach — sand foreground, ocean horizon, clear open sky
- texture: natural sand grain, clear ocean, crisp cloth on family clothing, soft cloud wisps

## F. Subject Treatment
- posing: candid family moment — one parent kneeling, child pointing upward, other parent standing — genuine joy
- scale: proportional — family occupies ~40% canvas area
- cropping: full figures visible from feet to head, no cropped limbs

## G. Text-Image Relationship
- pattern: card-on-scene — a white rounded rectangle card sits over the left side of the scene to carry product details
- contrast_strategy: pure white card surface for navy body text; the floating headline uses deep navy that reads directly over the pale sky

## H. Effects Library
- gradients: subtle horizontal sky-to-sand gradient in the photo only; no overlay gradients on typography or cards
- glows: none
- strokes: check-mark circles use solid fill only, no stroke
- drop_shadows: very soft drop behind the floating headline (rgba navy 15%, offset 0 2); the product card has soft rgba black at ~8% for subtle elevation

## I. Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Lean bright sky blue and clean white as the canvas
- Use yellow sparingly for energy punches (CTA and key emotional phrases)
- Frame real family lifestyle photography with genuine unscripted expressions
- Keep product details inside a clean white card, never directly overlaid on the photo
- Use green check-mark circles for feature bullets to preserve iconographic consistency

Don't:
- Don't use dark or moody backgrounds — this is a daytime system
- Don't introduce purple, gold, or metallic accents
- Don't crop the subjects tight — they must be full-body and connected to the environment
- Don't stack information density inside the card — use spaced bullets with icons
- Don't apply shallow depth of field; every grain of sand should be sharp
