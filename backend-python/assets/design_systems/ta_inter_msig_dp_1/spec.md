# Sunny Travel Joy

## A. Visual Mood
Joyful, vacation-anticipating, light, carefree. The energy is pre-trip excitement — bags packed, passport in hand, blue sky ahead. Mood keywords: joyful, vacation, sunny, carefree, light.

## B. Color Story
- dominant: #D7EAF6 (pale sky blue) and #F2DFB8 (warm sand beige) — environmental split between sky and foreground
- accent: #7B5BD1 (mid violet) — headline text, branded price callout box
- text_primary: #2B1F6B (deep purple-navy) — headline
- text_secondary: #4B3A9C (mid purple) — body copy
- shadow: none prominent — environment is bright and airy
- highlight: #FFC93C (sunny yellow) — CTA pill fill, price display numerals
- palette_relationship: analogous cool pale-sky with warm neutral sand, purple as the chromatic anchor, yellow as complementary punch for CTA and price only

## C. Typography Personality
- font_character: rounded friendly Thai sans, slightly softer than a standard UI sans, confident weights
- weight_hierarchy: headline 800, card body 500, price numerals 900 display, CTA 700
- tracking_behavior: normal; no compression at display sizes
- text_treatments: purple rounded card-pill for price callout block; yellow display numerals on purple inside that callout; no strokes; no shadows except faint card elevation

## D. Composition Archetype
- focal_pattern: rule-of-thirds — model anchored right-bottom two-thirds, white product card middle-left, title banner top
- text_zones: title band top 25%, product card middle-left, model right, CTA bottom-right
- negative_space_ratio: ~25% — sky gives generous breathing room above
- subject_position: lower-right third, leaning on suitcase

## E. Scene / Photo Style
- lighting_direction + quality: bright natural daytime sun from upper-left, soft warm, gentle, no harsh shadows
- depth: medium — foreground subject sharp, sky slightly soft toward horizon
- environment: outdoor beach sand with travel props (suitcase, sunhat, passport); sky with wispy clouds
- texture: sand grain, matte suitcase surface, woven sunhat, smooth skin

## F. Subject Treatment
- posing: single subject leaning lifestyle pose, smiling, leaning on suitcase with travel documents in hand, eye-level three-quarter angle
- scale: proportional half-body — subject occupies about 40% of canvas width
- cropping: half-body crop at hip, full hat in frame, suitcase fully visible

## G. Text-Image Relationship
- pattern: card-on-scene with a secondary purple pill-box for price
- contrast_strategy: pure white card over scene for body + a purple small card over scene for price punch + yellow-on-purple numerals + purple headline directly on pale sky

## H. Effects Library
- gradients: sky natural photographic gradient only; no overlay gradients on typography
- glows: none
- strokes: orange check-mark rings in outline style (2-3px) for feature bullets
- drop_shadows: cards have soft rgba black at ~10% for elevation; no shadows on body text

## I. Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Use pale sky blue and warm sand as the canvas split
- Purple is the chromatic anchor — used on headlines and the price callout box
- Yellow is strictly for CTA pill and price display numerals inside the purple box
- Use lifestyle photography showing a single smiling subject in candid travel action
- Include travel props (suitcase, hat, passport) as visible anchors to the theme

Don't:
- Don't use dark or moody backgrounds
- Don't drop the purple — this system requires its purple chromatic anchor
- Don't skip the purple price callout box — it is the branded visual handle
- Don't crop the hat or the suitcase prop — they define the travel context
- Don't use deep royal purple or metallic accents — purple here is mid-violet, not institutional
