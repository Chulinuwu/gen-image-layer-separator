# Dreamy Pastel Aspiration

## A. Visual Mood
Warm, hopeful, aspirational, maternal-tender. The atmosphere is a soft dream-space: a quiet moment between mother and child where future possibilities float around them as visible, tangible bubbles carrying life-goal icons. Energy is gentle-optimistic, not loud or stoic; emotional and intimate. Mood keywords: hopeful, nurturing, dreamy, tender, aspirational.

## B. Color Story
- dominant: #A98DD9 (lavender-violet wash) — the environmental tint that bathes the entire scene, including skin and fabric
- accent: #E8B94F (soft gold) — used exclusively on emphasis numerals and headline accents
- text_primary: #FFFFFF (pure white) — all headline copy over the photo
- text_secondary: #E7DFF5 (pale lilac) — supporting body copy
- shadow: #3B1F66 (deep purple) — used only as soft text shadow and bubble rim
- highlight: #FFD862 (lemon yellow) — primary CTA pill background and promo numeral fill
- palette_relationship: analogous (violet to lavender to lilac) anchored by a warm gold complementary accent; no greens, no cool blues

## C. Typography Personality
- font_character: heavy Thai sans display with compact letterforms, friendly-rounded corners yet structured body
- weight_hierarchy: headline 800 display, emphasis numerals 700 with gradient fill, body 500, fine print 400
- tracking_behavior: negative at display (-2 to -3%), normal at body
- text_treatments: soft drop shadow on all white headline text for contrast against the photo-mixed background; gold-gradient fill on the product-term numerals ("85/8"); yellow solid fill on promo percentages inside column body; no stroke; no outline-only treatments

## D. Composition Archetype
- focal_pattern: rule-of-thirds — subject mass on the right third, text on the left and top
- text_zones: title band top 20%, product offer band middle 15%, benefit columns split 50/50 below it, CTA strip bottom 10%
- negative_space_ratio: ~25% — busy but not crowded; floating bubbles and bokeh fill without clutter
- subject_position: right-of-center, lower two-thirds

## E. Scene / Photo Style
- lighting_direction + quality: warm diffuse window light from upper-right, soft falloff creating gentle shadows; natural daytime indoor
- depth: shallow DoF, background bokeh melts into soft purple haze
- environment: indoor lifestyle — bedroom setting with soft fabric and plant silhouettes
- texture: soft-smooth skin, fabric highlights, airy pastel grain

## F. Subject Treatment
- posing: intimate candid — mother and child lying on a bed, faces close, genuine laughter
- scale: proportional — subjects occupy about 40% of canvas height
- cropping: waist-up on mother, full torso on child, both lying horizontal

## G. Text-Image Relationship
- pattern: overlay-integrated — text sits directly on the scene, the uniform purple tint of the scene provides inherent contrast for white text
- contrast_strategy: soft drop shadow behind white text and inherent background-tint contrast; no scrim, no card

## H. Effects Library
- gradients: vertical gold gradient on display numerals (#F4C744 top to #B88A28 bottom); soft purple vignette at edges
- glows: subtle luminous edges on floating bubbles; faint halo around bubble interiors
- strokes: none
- drop_shadows: soft 0 4 12 rgba(30, 10, 60, 0.35) behind all white headline text

## I. Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Keep the pastel lavender environmental tint dominant and uniform across the entire scene
- Use gold gradient only for product-term numerals, not for general emphasis
- Preserve soft diffuse natural window light; no ring-light or harsh flash
- Frame family-focused lifestyle photography with genuine unscripted emotion
- Use floating bubble metaphor with translucent containers for conceptual icons

Don't:
- Don't use saturated, neon, or cool-blue palettes — this system is pastel violet
- Don't use hard shadows or high-contrast studio lighting
- Don't let the photo dominate the floating dream icons — they are visual co-heroes
- Don't place text on the subjects' faces or over their eyes
- Don't introduce teal, green, or red accents; gold is the only warm accent
