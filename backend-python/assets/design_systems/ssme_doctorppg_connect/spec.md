# Chrome Purple Prestige

## A. Visual Mood
Prestigious, professional-authority, premium-credit, confident-serious. The visual language for high-value credit offered to established professionals — no playfulness, no lifestyle warmth, just sculpted authority rendered in chrome. Mood keywords: prestigious, professional, authoritative, premium, serious.

## B. Color Story
- dominant: #3E1F68 (deep purple) to #6B3DA8 (mid violet) — gradient background
- accent: metallic purple-silver gradient (#A08FC8 to #E9DEF8) — chrome reflective treatment on 3D numerals and wordmark
- text_primary: #FFFFFF (pure white) — sub-labels and body
- text_secondary: #D0C5E5 (light lilac) — fine print and rate disclaimer
- shadow: #16073A (near-black purple) — deep vignette and numeral drop shadows
- highlight: #EADDFF (icy lilac) — specular reflections on chrome letterforms
- palette_relationship: monochromatic purple across multiple lightness steps; metallic chrome gradient is the only accent treatment, not a chromatic deviation

## C. Typography Personality
- font_character: massive 3D chrome extruded display for primary numerals and wordmark; bold Thai sans for sub-labels and body
- weight_hierarchy: 3D display headliner is a rendered object — weight is inherent to its extrusion; sub-labels 600; body 400
- tracking_behavior: tight on 3D numerals (they touch optical boundaries); normal on flat Thai body
- text_treatments: 3D extrusion with metallic chrome reflection, beveled edges, gradient fill, subtle inner highlight on letter faces, soft drop shadow grounding the numerals; all other text rendered flat white sans

## D. Composition Archetype
- focal_pattern: centered typographic hero — the 3D numerals and wordmark ARE the composition; all other elements are support
- text_zones: top quarter for brand and product label, middle half for 3D display numerals, bottom quarter for rate details and CTA
- negative_space_ratio: ~15% — numerals carry heavy visual mass and crowd toward dense
- subject_position: dead-center, typography-forward

## E. Scene / Photo Style
- lighting_direction + quality: clinical-pharmaceutical diffuse on blurred doctor backdrop; strong directional studio light on 3D numerals creating chrome specular highlights
- depth: extreme shallow DoF — doctors are heavy blur so they do not compete with numerals
- environment: studio with doctor group as abstract backdrop, not a real place
- texture: hyper-polished chrome metal on numerals, soft clinical white coats, flat purple gradient field

## F. Subject Treatment
- posing: three doctors in arms-crossed or stethoscope pose, out of focus, centered
- scale: backdrop — occupies full canvas width but severely blurred
- cropping: framed tight at torso level, faces mostly out of frame or blurred beyond recognition

## G. Text-Image Relationship
- pattern: typography-IS-hero — the scene is atmospheric backdrop, typography is the actual subject; inversion of lifestyle and card-on-scene patterns
- contrast_strategy: 3D chrome numerals have inherent highlight-to-shadow self-contrast that reads on any purple; white flat body text reads on deep purple directly

## H. Effects Library
- gradients: radial purple gradient on background; vertical chrome gradient on 3D numerals (dark base to light top); specular highlight accents on polished edges
- glows: soft purple ambient glow around the edges of 3D numerals
- strokes: none on Thai body; 3D numerals have self-bevel edges as their "stroke"
- drop_shadows: deep purple drop under 3D numerals to ground them on the gradient field

## I. Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Let the numerals and wordmark be the actual hero, not human subjects
- Use 3D chrome treatment only on credit amounts and the product wordmark
- Keep professionals as softly blurred context, not sharp hero subjects
- Maintain monochromatic purple discipline across background and metallic accents
- Frame the CTA as a thin line-art graphic, not a filled pill button

Don't:
- Don't bring the human subjects into sharp focus — they compete with the numerals
- Don't introduce warm accents (gold, yellow, orange) into this system
- Don't use lifestyle, outdoor, or consumer-warm photography
- Don't flatten the numerals to 2D — the 3D chrome is the signature
- Don't use pill-button CTAs — the visual language is line-framed, typography-forward
