# Chrome Purple Prestige — SCB Business Lending for Doctors

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

This is a prestige-credit aesthetic built on **monochromatic deep purple** with **3D chrome metallic numerals** as the signature treatment. Three out-of-focus doctors in white coats stand as an atmospheric backdrop while massive 3D extruded chrome purple letters and numerals — the wordmark "[product-category wordmark, all caps, 1 English word]" and the credit amounts "[primary credit amount numeral]" and "[repayment period numeral]" — dominate the composition as the actual hero subjects. Typography is literally the product: the bank is saying "we quantify trust in millions, and this is what it looks like."

The energy is **prestigious, professional-authority, premium-credit, confident-serious**. No lifestyle warmth, no bright daylight, no playful accent colors. Lighting is clinical-pharmaceutical on the blurred professionals behind, and dramatic studio on the chrome numerals creating crisp specular highlights and beveled edges. The CTA is rendered as a thin-line framed graphic rather than a filled pill — pills belong to consumer-facing systems, and this is decidedly not that.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Monochromatic deep purple background with gradient radial toward center
- Three out-of-focus doctors in white coats as atmospheric backdrop — not hero subjects
- Massive 3D chrome purple extruded wordmark "[product-category wordmark, all caps, 1 English word]" centered upper-mid
- Massive 3D chrome purple extruded numerals "[primary credit amount numeral]" and "[repayment period numeral]" for credit amounts
- Thai sub-labels in flat white sans for scale and duration units
- Brand lockup top-left: SCB logo + "BUSINESS Lending" side label
- Subtitle "[subtitle describing product category and target segment]" beneath wordmark
- Rate details and disclaimer in mid-lower body in gray/light-lilac
- **Line-art framed CTA** bottom-right, not a pill button
- Date validity range top-right area
- Extreme shallow depth of field on the doctor backdrop
- No lifestyle warmth, no real environment, no warm accent colors

## 3. Typography Rules

### Font Family
- **3D Chrome Display**: a rendered object, not a font choice — built on a heavy condensed caps display base, extruded with beveled edges and polished chrome gradient material
- **Primary Thai sans**: bold sans for sub-labels, subtitles, and body, same family across weights
- **No serif**, **no script**, **no secondary sans**

### Hierarchy

| Role | Weight | Size Category | Tracking | Line Height | Treatment | Color |
|------|--------|---------------|----------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| 3D Chrome Wordmark ("[product-category wordmark, all caps, 1 English word]") | N/A (rendered 3D) | Display XXL (~100-140px visual) | Tight (glyphs kiss) | N/A | 3D extrusion, beveled edges, chrome gradient fill, soft purple outer glow, drop shadow | Gradient (Mid Light → Mid Purple → Deep Face) |
| 3D Chrome Numerals ("[primary credit amount numeral]", "[repayment period numeral]") | N/A (rendered 3D) | Display XXL (~120-160px visual) | Tight | N/A | Same 3D treatment as wordmark, possibly with sharper specular on top faces | Same gradient |
| Sub-label (small, scale-units like "[million-baht scale unit]", "[year unit with asterisk]") | 500 | Body (~12-16px) | Normal | 1.3 | None | `#FFFFFF` |
| "[scale label above primary credit amount]" / "[scale label above repayment period]" | 500-600 | Body (~14-18px) | Normal | 1.3 | None | `#FFFFFF` |
| Subtitle ("[subtitle describing product category and target segment]") | 700 | Body-L (~20-26px) | Normal | 1.3 | None | `#FFFFFF` |
| Rate Body Paragraph | 500 | Body-S (~12-14px) | Normal | 1.5 | None | `#D0C5E5` |
| Date Validity | 600 | Body (~14-16px) | Normal | 1.3 | None | `#FFFFFF` |
| CTA Framed Label | 700 | Body-L (~16-20px) | Normal | 1.3 | None | `#FFFFFF` |
| Fine Print | 400 | Micro (~9-10px) | Normal | 1.4 | None | `#AA9BCF` |

### Principles
- **The 3D display is the headline**. It replaces any traditional display typeface; it is a rendered object, not a typeset string.
- **Flat white Thai sans handles everything else** — no secondary display treatment, no gradient fills on body text.
- **Tracking on 3D numerals is tight** — glyphs can kiss at the baseline for weight.
- **No drop shadow on flat body text** — the monochrome purple background provides inherent contrast with white.
- **CTA label is 700 weight, not italicized or stylized** — the drama comes from the surrounding line-art frame, not the label text.

## 4. Component & Element Stylings

### 3D Chrome Display (Wordmark + Numerals)
- Rendered as 3D extruded objects with PBR metallic material
- Material: high metallic (1.0), low roughness (~0.1-0.2), purple-tinted chrome IOR
- Extrusion depth: moderate (~12-20% of letter height)
- Bevel on front face edges: small but crisp, ~1-2% of letter height
- Chrome gradient: top face bright `#EADDFF`, lower face shadow `#5A3D96`, side face deepest `#2A1453`
- Specular highlights: sharp dots on leading edges where key light hits
- Soft purple outer glow (~8-12px spread, `#A08FC8` at ~20% opacity)
- Drop shadow: deep purple `0 12 40 rgba(22, 7, 58, 0.50)` to ground the letters against background

### Wordmark Specific — "[product-category wordmark, all caps, 1 English word]"
- The letter D contains or anchors a small integrated stethoscope graphic inside its counter or alongside it (brand detail unique to this system)
- Treated as a single horizontal unit, all caps, condensed
- Letters kiss at baselines for compressed weight
- Approximately 7 letters wide, occupying ~55-65% canvas width

### Credit Numerals — "[primary credit amount numeral]" and "[repayment period numeral]"
- Each numeral pair (total value + repayment period) gets the same 3D chrome treatment
- Paired with small white Thai sub-labels ("[million-baht scale unit]" next to 50, "[year unit with asterisk]" next to 10)
- Sub-labels sit at the baseline of the numerals, slightly above
- Numerals are positioned in opposing horizontal halves of the canvas (50 left-of-center, 10 right-of-center) or stacked with separators

### Sub-Labels Around Numerals
- Small white Thai sans, vertical-right-aligned next to the numeral
- Example: "[credit amount numeral + scale unit]" — the asterisk footnote marker is inline
- "[scale label above primary credit amount]" label sits above or beside each numeral as a mini caption
- "[scale label above repayment period]" label sits next to the period numeral

### Subtitle Line
- Single line of Thai sans 700 white, ~22px
- Position: just below the 3D wordmark, left-aligned or centered
- Color: pure white
- No drop shadow

### Doctor Backdrop (Blurred)
- Three adult subjects in white coats arranged horizontally centered
- Poses: at least one with arms crossed, at least one with stethoscope visible around neck, at least one with visible wristwatch
- Extreme shallow DoF — faces heavily blurred (subjects non-identifiable); body forms and coat outlines readable
- Lighting: soft diffuse cool-clinical light source (~5500K tinted toward purple), no warm cast
- Occupies full canvas width at the same vertical zone as the 3D numerals, intentionally behind them

### Line-Art Framed CTA (Bottom-Right)
- Thin 1-2px white line-art rectangle frame with curved/rounded inner corners
- Frame is rectangular, not pill-shaped
- Inside the frame: short Thai phrase "[2-line framed-CTA value proposition]" in 2 lines
- Text: Thai sans 700 white
- No fill color inside the frame — it is outline-only
- This line-art treatment is the system's distinctive CTA style (not a pill, not a button)

### SCB + BUSINESS Lending Lockup (Top-Left)
- SCB logo primary, small, top-left margin
- Next to SCB: small square or label "BUSINESS Lending" in light purple `#9B7FCC` or white
- May include a book/ledger icon or abstract mark

### Date Validity
- Short text range "1 ม.ค. 68 - 31 ธ.ค. 68" in Thai sans 600 white
- Position: top-right, horizontal alignment with SCB lockup
- No frame or pill around it

### Rate Details Paragraph
- Multi-line body paragraph in light lilac `#D0C5E5`, Thai sans 500 ~13px
- Explains rate structure as a multi-line paragraph with starting and maximum rate references (e.g. "MRR-n.n% to MRR+n.n% per year depending on collateral status")
- Sits in mid-lower canvas area
- Left-aligned, ~60-75% canvas width

### Fine Print Disclaimer
- Bottom strip, Thai sans 400 ~10px in muted lilac `#AA9BCF`
- Explains MRR, terms, reference to website
- Left-aligned or justified, full content-width

## 5. Layout Principles

### Vertical Zone Map (1000px canvas reference)
- **Top 0-12%**: SCB + BUSINESS Lending lockup top-left; date validity top-right
- **12-32%**: 3D wordmark "[product-category wordmark, all caps, 1 English word]" centered
- **32-40%**: subtitle line (single-line Thai sans 700)
- **40-70%**: 3D chrome numerals + sub-labels (50 left side, 10 right side) with doctor backdrop
- **70-85%**: rate details paragraph (body) + date range info
- **85-95%**: line-art framed CTA bottom-right
- **95-100%**: fine print disclaimer strip

### Horizontal Strategy
- Wordmark centered horizontally
- Numerals split left/right of canvas center for scale comparison
- Sub-labels attach to each numeral
- CTA frame positioned bottom-right
- SCB lockup and date are balanced at top-left and top-right

### Negative Space
- ~15% of canvas is pure gradient purple field — tight, dense layout
- The 3D numerals claim heavy visual mass
- Text body paragraph is the only place where ample body-copy breathing room is needed

### Spacing Scale
- Base unit: 8px
- Section gaps: 24-40px
- Numeral spacing to sub-label: 12-16px
- CTA frame internal padding: 12-16px
- Line-art stroke width: 1-2px (crisp)

### Safe Zones
- 3D wordmark and numerals claim priority — no other element may overlap them
- Doctor backdrop must stay out of focus — never pulled into sharpness
- CTA frame must not collide with fine print strip
- Rate paragraph sits on uncontested background

## 6. Photography / 3D Rendering Rules

### Rendering Approach
- **Hybrid**: photographic subjects (doctors) composited with 3D rendered chrome typography
- Doctors photographed or stock-sourced in a controlled studio with white-coat wardrobe
- 3D typography rendered separately and composited in front of the blurred photographic layer

### Lighting — Doctor Layer
- Soft diffuse fill from multiple directions
- Color temperature slightly cool, then color-graded toward purple
- Doctors are a group shot, blurred through post (or shot with a wide aperture like f/1.4)

### Lighting — 3D Chrome Layer
- Single dramatic directional key from upper-right
- Secondary fill from lower-left at ~30% intensity to catch the bottom face
- Environmental reflection map: purple gradient (matches background) so the chrome looks natively integrated

### Depth & Focus
- Extreme shallow DoF on the doctor layer — faces unrecognizable
- 3D layer is in sharp focus (rendered, not photographed)
- No motion blur, no atmospheric haze

### Color Grading
- Heavy monochrome purple push across the composite
- White coats shifted warm-cool depending on highlight direction
- Chrome saturated toward the mid-violet range to match environment

### Composition
- Doctors arranged horizontally, slightly behind center
- Three doctors: one male, one female, one additional (hands visible, watch/stethoscope cues)
- None of the doctors' faces should be fully legible — identity is irrelevant, presence is the point

### No Real Environment
- There is no hospital, no office, no context beyond a blurred group
- Background is a pure color field, not an environment

## 7. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Build on monochromatic deep purple gradient with radial lighter center
- Render the headline and credit amounts as 3D chrome extruded objects with purple-tinted metallic material
- Photograph or source a group of white-coat doctors as a heavily blurred atmospheric backdrop
- Use flat white Thai sans for all non-3D text
- Render the CTA as a thin line-art rectangular frame, not a pill button
- Use extreme shallow depth of field on the doctor layer
- Reserve icy lilac highlights for chrome specular only, not for text color
- Place the 3D typography as the actual hero subject — it is the product
- Keep the doctor layer blurred even at preview size — any sharpening reveals identity and breaks the abstract-prestige register
- Include a date validity range top-right for campaign period

### Don't
- Don't introduce warm colors (gold, yellow, orange) into this system
- Don't use lifestyle or outdoor photography — this is a studio-blurred abstract backdrop
- Don't bring the doctors into sharp focus — they compete with the numerals
- Don't flatten the numerals to 2D — the 3D chrome is the signature
- Don't use pill-button CTAs — line-art framed CTA is the branded style
- Don't add warm-gold chrome or silver chrome — the chrome is purple-tinted specifically
- Don't use Thai sans italics or secondary display fonts — body text is flat sans
- Don't use check-mark icons — there are no feature bullets in this system
- Don't use pastel or light-mode backgrounds — purple depth is the canvas
- Don't include product cards — the layout is typography-on-backdrop, not card-on-scene
- Don't crowd the 3D typography with competing graphic elements

### Iteration Guide
1. If the chrome numerals look "silver" or "neutral", the material IOR is wrong — push it toward purple-tinted chrome specifically
2. If the doctors' faces are recognizable at preview size, increase the blur — identity is not the point, atmospheric presence is
3. If the composition feels "loose" or "consumer", it has too much negative space — tighten toward a dense typographic hero
4. Chrome specular highlights must be crisp and small — if they are soft and large, the key light is too diffuse
5. The purple background gradient should have a visible lighter center — a flat purple field reads as "less dramatic"
6. If the CTA looks like a button, re-render as pure line-art with no fill — the frame is the distinctive signature
7. The 3D extrusion depth should be moderate — too deep and it reads as kitsch, too shallow and it reads as flat
8. If warm colors creep in (gold, yellow, orange), the system has been contaminated — purge entirely and re-render
